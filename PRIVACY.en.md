# Privacy notice

[简体中文](PRIVACY.md) · English · [Product overview](README.en.md)

Updated: September 12, 2026. Applicable version: Rynoa v1.0.0.

## Current free release

- Action bindings, app location information, and launch-at-login, action-feedback, and pause preferences are stored on this Mac.
- Recent selections retain only the four most recently selected distinct apps to make choosing actions faster.
- Only shortcuts you configure are saved. Rynoa does not record what you type.
- Body-sensor signals are used for real-time recognition. Raw signals are not saved or uploaded.
- Usage analytics, diagnostic reports, and crash logs are not uploaded automatically.
- Rynoa connects to GitHub for the public release list only when you manually check for updates. The request includes the app version, and GitHub receives ordinary network connection information. Bindings, installed-app lists, and license keys are not sent to the update endpoint.
- Help, feedback, and email links open your browser or email app. Information you submit is handled by the relevant service. Public issues are unsuitable for private information.

## Free use

The current free release does not call trial, license, or payment APIs. No account, purchase, or activation is required. Keyboard gestures, body taps, and action bindings work offline. If paid access becomes available, the privacy notice and release announcements will be updated first.

## Contact

For help removing local settings or handling information you have provided through support, contact [kimjinso@qq.com](mailto:kimjinso@qq.com). Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove local preferences.
