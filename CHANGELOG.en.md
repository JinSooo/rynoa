## v1.0.0-beta.8 — September 11, 2026

App version 1.0.0 (8). Free public beta.

- Adds an English interface for menus, settings, permissions, action selection, feedback, and help. Rynoa follows the macOS language by default; choose English or Simplified Chinese for Rynoa in System Settings, then fully quit and reopen the app to apply it.
- Adds an English website and public documentation, with English app screenshots and language-switching instructions.
- Preserves existing action bindings and preferences. The beta remains free, works offline, and requires no activation.

This build continues to use development signing and has not completed Developer ID distribution signing or Apple notarization. Further testing is still needed on other Mac models, real sleep/lock scenarios, multiple displays, and long-term body-tap use.
