# Third-party notices

PalmTap Lab independently implements an experimental AppleSPU HID reader using device identifiers and report-layout research published by these MIT-licensed projects:

- `olvvier/apple-silicon-accelerometer` — https://github.com/olvvier/apple-silicon-accelerometer
